F. Correct the Errors

Read the article, then rewrite each sentence correctly using information from the article.

Home Remedies

A Cold: Many people eat hot chicken soup. Other people drink water with red pepper, sugar, lemon juice, and milk.

A Headache: Put a cold cloth on your head, or splash your face with cold water. You can also put your hands in hot water. This can help.

A Cough: Drink warm tea or take some honey. This can make your throat feel better.

Insomnia: Drink a large glass of warm milk. Or take a very warm bath.

Example: Use these easy remedies at work.
Corrected: Use these easy remedies at home.
1. For a cold, some people eat hot pepper soup or drink hot water.
2. For a cough, you can drink warm milk or take some honey.
3. For a headache, put a little hot water on your face.
4. For insomnia, drink warm tea or take a warm bath.
