D. Wh-Questions

Write questions to complete the conversations. Use the words in parentheses.

Example: Where do you play tennis? (play / tennis)

2. A: (play / soccer)
B: I play soccer with my school team.
3. A: (your team / practice)
B: We practice five days a week.
